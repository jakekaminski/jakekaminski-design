## Overview

Radicle Agronomics built the world's first fully automated soil sampling lab —
and launched their product publicly without a public-facing website. In early
2024 they needed to ship one fast, built from scratch, with no design system,
no existing content, and no precedent for how to explain automated soil science
to farmers and dealers.

## Process

### Parallel design and content sprints

In most projects I wait for final copy before high-fidelity design. Here, that
wasn't possible — content and design had to move in parallel. I built the design
system against placeholder content structures, then swapped in real copy as it
was approved. This kept both tracks moving without blocking each other.

<ImageWithCaption
    src="/images/radag-page-templates.png"
    alt="Page wireframe templates"
    caption="Wireframe templates established content structure before final copy was written."
/>

### Architecture: Next.js + Chakra UI + Kontent.ai

The stack choices were driven by timeline and maintainability. Next.js for
performance and SEO, Chakra UI for a component system the team could extend
without a designer in every loop, and Kontent.ai for content management so the
client could update pages without touching code. HubSpot was integrated for
lead capture.

### Design system from scratch

With no prior web design system to inherit, I built one from Radicle's brand
guidelines — color tokens, type scale, spacing system, and a component library
that covered all page types needed for launch.

<ImageWithCaption
    src="/images/radag-design-system.png"
    alt="Radicle Agronomics web design system"

/>

## Outcome

Launched in early 2024. The site gave Radicle a professional digital presence
that matched the seriousness of their technology. Lead capture was operational
from day one, with HubSpot CRM sync. The CMS setup meant the client could manage
all content independently after handoff.
