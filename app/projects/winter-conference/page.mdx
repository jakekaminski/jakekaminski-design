The logo animation served as the splash screen between sessions at Precision
Planting's Winter Conference — their largest event of the year. Designed in
Adobe After Effects, exported as an H.264 MP4, then embedded via HTML5 `<video>`
with `autoplay`, `loop`, and `muted` attributes for seamless in-event playback.

The motion design brief required matching the conference's energy without
competing with the content — bold reveal on logo, clean hold, smooth exit.

## Landing Page

![Winter Conference landing page](/images/wc25-landing-page.png)

The conference landing page was built on the existing Precision Planting
infrastructure: **Next.js + Chakra UI**, deployed to their Vercel environment.
The page covered event registration (map-based venue nav), speaker bios,
session schedule, and FAQ — all content-managed through Kontent.ai so the
events team could make updates without a dev.
